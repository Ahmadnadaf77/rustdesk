          Go to: Settings → Actions → Enable Actions

Problem: "Workflow fails or times out"
Solution: Just click "Re-run all jobs" - it happens sometimes

Solution: The file is 200-300 MB, use a good internet connection
          Download might take 5-15 minutes depending on speed

Solution: You downloaded the wrong thing
          Make sure to download "rustdesk-windows-installer"
          from the Artifacts section, not something else

Problem: "Extracted folder is only 20-30 MB"
Solution: Something went wrong in the build
          Re-run the GitHub Actions workflow
          Or check the workflow logs for errors
